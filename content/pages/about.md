## Colophon

This blog is built with Rust (axum + Askama + htmx) and served from a
single statically-linked binary sandboxed with Linux landlock, fronted by
Caddy for TLS. Posts are Markdown files rendered at startup and served from
memory. Source is available under the MIT License.
